Account Deletion – Biggi Data

Users can request deletion of their Biggi Data account by sending an email to support@yourdomain.com using their registered email address.

Once verified, we will permanently delete:

  • User profile information
  • Wallet data
  • Game activity and reward history
  • Some transaction records may be retained for up to 90 days or longer where required by law for fraud prevention and financial compliance.
